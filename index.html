Common questions

Will I be riding in my own car?

Yes. You ride as the passenger while one of our drivers takes the wheel of your vehicle, so you wake up with your car in your own driveway — nothing to go back for the next morning.

How much will my ride cost?

Trips under 10 km are one flat price of $39.99 with no booking fee. Trips of 11 km or more are a $19.99 turn-on key fee plus $2.50 per kilometre. Dispatch confirms your exact total on the phone before anyone is sent out.

How soon can a team reach me?

In most cases a team is with you in 15 to 30 minutes. The exact timing depends on where you are and on traffic.

Can I reserve a driver ahead of time?

Absolutely — call ahead for any night out, and hosts can pre-book a driver to stay on standby for a party, wedding or company event. Weekends and December dates fill up quickly.
